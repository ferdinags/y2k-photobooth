PIXEL POP ★ Retro Help & Guide

📸 Webcam Capture: Stand in good lighting, click "Snap 4 Photos", and strike 4 cute poses during the countdown!

📁 Upload Fallback: If you don't have a webcam, upload up to 4 photos from your phone or laptop.

🎨 Scrapbook Tools: Click any sticker to slap it onto the strip. Drag to move, grab the top stem to rotate, or drag the yellow corner to resize!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts: Delete/Backspace to delete element. Arrow keys to nudge. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y for Undo/Redo.

🔒 Privacy: Everything runs locally in your browser cache. No photos are ever uploaded to any cloud server.
